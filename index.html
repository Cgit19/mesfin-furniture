Our Story

Craftsmanship Built on Passion and Experience.

Mesfin Furniture was founded with a clear purpose: to bring quality Ethiopian furniture craftsmanship into homes across Addis Ababa and Shaggar City. Working from our workshop in Burayu Ketta, we combine traditional woodworking skills with modern design sensibility.

Over the past 5+ years, we've had the privilege of furnishing homes with pieces that our customers are proud of. We take each order seriously — listening carefully to what the customer needs, selecting the right materials, and delivering work that lasts.

Whether you're looking for a classic bed frame, a sleek wardrobe, a sturdy dining set, or a fully custom kitchen cabinet — we have the skills and dedication to make it happen.
